Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Ciel Kwon is an independent Discord bot designed to help players of The Sims 4 investigate errors, potential mod conflicts, and custom-content issues through diagnostic file analysis.

1. Information we process

When you use Ciel Kwon, we may process:

  • LastException text files you voluntarily upload.
  • Error messages, filenames, mod references, file paths, and other diagnostic details within those files.
  • Discord interaction information needed to receive commands and send responses.

Diagnostic files can contain potentially personal information, such as computer usernames or file paths. Review your files before uploading them.

2. How information is used

We use this information to analyze Sims 4 exception reports, identify possible error patterns and affected resources, provide troubleshooting suggestions, and maintain bot functionality. We do not sell personal information or intentionally collect it for advertising.

3. Public reports

The /scan command posts diagnostic results in the Discord channel where it is used. Members with access to that channel may see the report, including filenames or other diagnostic details extracted from the uploaded file. The original uploaded file is not intentionally reposted by the bot as part of its diagnostic response.

4. Storage and retention

The current bot is designed to process uploaded files in memory to produce a report, rather than intentionally saving the original uploaded file in a persistent database. Reports posted to Discord may remain accessible according to Discord’s message retention and deletion practices. The hosting environment may create operational logs.

Operator check before publishing: Confirm that your deployed bot does not store uploads or additional user data, and that your operational logs match this description. Update this section if your implementation changes.

5. Third-party services

Ciel Kwon operates through Discord. Discord’s own terms and privacy practices also apply to use of its platform.

6. Security

We take reasonable steps to protect information processed by the bot, but no online service can guarantee complete security.

7. Your choices and requests

Using Ciel Kwon is voluntary. You can choose not to upload files. For privacy questions or requests concerning data under the operator’s control, contact us through our Discord support server. Please do not post sensitive personal information in public channels.

8. Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the bot’s features or data practices change. The current version will be published on this page.
